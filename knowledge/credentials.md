## The signed consent comes first

Portal logins are kept only once the client's signed consent and power of attorney is on file. Open the client, choose the Files tab, set the category to Signed consent and power of attorney, and upload the signed copy. Until then the Credentials tab says what is missing.

## Saving a portal login

On the client's Credentials tab each government portal is a row: Income Tax, GST, MCA21, TRACES, e-invoicing, e-waybill and professional tax. Choose Add or Edit on a row, enter the user ID and password, and save. Leaving the password blank when editing keeps the one stored.

## Seeing a password

Passwords are never shown in the list. Someone allowed to reveal them chooses Reveal, gives the reason (for example the return being filed), and the password is shown for 30 seconds. Every reveal is recorded with who, when and why, and listed under Who revealed what. Someone without that permission can ask a partner for temporary access.

## Client files

The Files tab keeps a client's documents: drop them in or choose them, up to 25 MB each. Files always download rather than opening in the browser. When a client is permanently removed, its files are removed too.
