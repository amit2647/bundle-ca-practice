## What the CA Practice bundle adds

The CA Practice bundle sets the workspace up for a chartered accountancy firm. It adds the firm's service catalog (statutory audit, tax audit, GST returns, income tax, TDS, ROC and payroll services, plus packages such as company annual compliance), role templates for Partner, Audit Manager, Article Assistant and Accounts Executive, and deadline reminder emails.

## Reminder emails start switched off

The bundle installs two reminder emails — one before a deadline and one after it is missed. They send real email to clients, so they stay off until someone turns them on in Settings, then Email Automations.

## Editing what the bundle installed

Services, roles and email templates installed by the bundle can be edited like any other. When a newer version of the bundle arrives, anything you edited is kept as you left it, and Settings, then Bundle, shows that a newer version is available for you to review.
