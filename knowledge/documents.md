## Where letters are made

Open a client and choose the Documents tab. Pick the financial year at the top: there is a tile for each letter the firm issues. A tile that is faded does not apply to this client that year, and says why — for example the tax audit engagement letter needs Tax Audit engaged for the year, and the letter to the previous auditor needs a previous auditor recorded on the engagement.

## Drafting and finalizing a letter

Click a tile to open the letter. The firm's details at the top come from Settings, then Firm. Fill in the letter's fields — some start filled from the client and the year's engagement, such as the fee — and choose Apply changes to see the letter. Save draft keeps it; a draft can be changed and deleted. Finalize freezes it, with an optional UDIN, and is refused while anything is still missing (shown as highlighted gaps). A finalized letter cannot be changed; Print prints it on the firm's letterhead.

## The firm's own wording

In Settings, Document templates, someone with settings access can edit a letter's text as the firm's own version and preview it with a client. Letters already made keep the text they were made from. Restore the bundle's text goes back to the original.
