## How deadlines are created

Deadlines are not typed in. When a client is engaged for a financial year, every service engaged that year produces its deadlines: GST returns give GSTR-1 on the 11th and GSTR-3B on the 20th of each following month, TDS gives four quarterly returns, ROC gives AOC-4 and MGT-7, and so on. Changing the services on the engagement adds or removes their deadlines. A deadline already marked filed is never changed.

## Due soon, overdue and filed

A deadline is overdue once its date has passed, and due soon in the 30 days before it. Mark a deadline In progress, Filed or Not applicable from the client's Compliance tab or the Deadlines page.

## Extended due dates

When the government extends a due date, a person who can manage deadline rules records the extension for that year in Settings, then Deadline rules. Open deadlines move to the new date; filed ones stay as they were.

## Reminders

The bundle installs two reminder emails — before a deadline and after it is missed — switched off. Turn them on in Settings, then Email Automations.
